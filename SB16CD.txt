
 1. About SB16CD

   SB16CD is a digital CD player for DOS. To play sounds, it will read
  audio tracks of a CD and copy the data to the sound buffer of the
  sound card.
   SB16CD expects to find a SoundBlaster 16 compatible sound card - it
  plays sound with 44.100 Hz, 16-bit and 2 channels.


 2. Usage

  SB16CD has a few command line options:

   -? :          display help
   -q :          be quiet
   -d:<driver>   call the CD-ROM driver directly, don't use MSCDEX/SHSUCDX

  If MSCDEX is used, ensure that CD-ROM access is NOT cached by SmartDrive -
  caching the audio data would require too much time, resulting in distorted
  playback.
